# PuraVita Lifesciences Website

A premium skincare brand website for PuraVita Lifesciences, an Indian brand with international standards.

## Features

- **Modern Design**: Clean lavender & white theme with premium aesthetics
- **Fully Responsive**: Mobile-first design that works on all devices
- **Multiple Pages**: Complete website with all requested pages
- **Optimized Performance**: Fast loading with minimal dependencies
- **GitHub Pages Ready**: Easy to deploy on GitHub Pages

## Pages Included

1. **Home** (`index.html`) - Main landing page with hero section and featured content
2. **About Us** (`about.html`) - Company information and philosophy
3. **Our Products** (`products.html`) - Product showcase and details
4. **Why PuraVita** (`why-puravita.html`) - Brand differentiators and benefits
5. **Ingredients Philosophy** (`ingredients.html`) - Ingredient selection approach
6. **Quality & Compliance** (`quality.html`) - Manufacturing standards and quality control
7. **Our Vision** (`vision.html`) - Company vision and mission
8. **Contact Us** (`contact.html`) - Contact form and company details

## Design Specifications

- **Colors**: Lavender, White, Soft Grey, Touch of Gold
- **Fonts**: Poppins (headings), Inter (body)
- **Tone**: Premium, trustworthy, scientific, modern
- **Layout**: Clean sections with smooth animations
